Transit contracts:
- Billing cycle: monthly (30 days). You are invoiced once per month.
- Expensive: $5.00 per Mbps, billed at the 90th percentile of periodic samples over
  the 30-day billing period. Dominant direction billing (ingress or egress, whichever
  is higher). Top 10% of samples discarded. Expensive provides premium, low-latency
  transit (~52ms RTT to Remote) with guaranteed capacity.
- Cheap: $1.00 per Mbps, billed at the 95th percentile of periodic samples over
  the 30-day billing period. Same dominant-direction billing. Top 5% of samples
  discarded. Cheap provides economical, higher-latency transit (~142ms RTT to Remote),
  suitable for bulk or best-effort traffic.

How percentile billing works:
Your provider measures traffic through your transit link at periodic intervals throughout
the billing period. At billing time, all samples are sorted by throughput. The top N% of
samples — the busiest intervals — are discarded. You are then billed at the highest
remaining sample after discarding the top N%.

Important: the discard window is per-provider and independent.

Traffic pattern:
TinyInc's traffic to Remote follows a known recurring pattern: approximately every
2 days, traffic spikes from the ~100 Mbps baseline to ~500 Mbps for a period of
several hours, then returns to baseline. You can rely on this pattern when planning
your routing strategy — do not wait to observe multiple spikes before acting on it.

Operational cadence:
Your control plane is invoked approximately every 6 hours. Each invocation is your
one opportunity to observe and act for that 6-hour window — the next invocation will
not arrive until 6 hours later. Traffic conditions can change significantly between
invocations (e.g. a traffic burst can begin and end within a single 6-hour window).

Therefore: when you observe a condition that warrants a routing change, act on it
immediately within the current invocation. Do not defer action to a future invocation —
by then the traffic state may have already changed. This applies in both directions:
- When you detect a spike, immediately route via Expensive.
- When the spike is gone, immediately route back via Cheap.


Goals:
- Minimise total transit cost over the billing period while maintaining full connectivity for TinyInc.
- Never declare yourself done or terminate. Cost optimisation is a continuous process:
  keep monitoring the billing clock and traffic, re-evaluate your routing decisions,
