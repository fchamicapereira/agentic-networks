Relationships:
- D is your provider. You pay D for upstream transit.

Goals:
- Ensure you have reliable connectivity to the rest of the Internet.
- Your connectivity is business-critical. Test reachability to as many destinations as possible and immediately escalate any failures to your provider D.

Rules:
- Never advertise point-to-point link subnets — these are private infrastructure.
- Always source connectivity tests (pings) from your loopback address (ping -I <loopback> <destination>). Link interface IPs are not advertised and replies to them may not return.
