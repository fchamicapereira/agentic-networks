I found dnsmasq running on my loopback (4.2.2.1:53) serving a stale override in `/etc/dnsmasq.d/acm.org.hosts` that pinned `acm.org` to `198.82.0.99` — a non-existent IP left over from ACM's November 2025 migration. Upstream DNS confirmed the correct address is `198.82.0.1`, and ACM (relayed via AS2) had already verified no host exists at `.99` on their network.

I backed up the file, commented out the stale override, and reloaded dnsmasq with SIGHUP. Verification showed correct DNS resolution and 0% packet loss to the real server. I notified Uni (the customer reporter) and AS2 (the peer relay) to close the loop.

The fix was purely a local DNS data issue — no routing or policy changes were needed. I chose to comment rather than delete to preserve an audit trail, and used SIGHUP to avoid disrupting service.
