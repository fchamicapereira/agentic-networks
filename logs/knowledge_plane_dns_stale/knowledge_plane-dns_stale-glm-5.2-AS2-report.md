I observed that 198.82.0.99 was unreachable (ICMP Host Unreachable from 198.82.0.254) while 198.82.0.1 and .254 were reachable from my loopback. My local audit confirmed AS2's routing and interfaces were correct, ruling out myself as the cause. I decided to forward the diagnostic request to ACM, since 198.82.0.254 is their gateway and they're my directly connected customer responsible for that segment. After ACM confirmed 198.82.0.99 doesn't exist (ARP failure, nmap showing only 2 alive hosts), I relayed their diagnosis back to AS1, which enabled AS1 to find and fix a stale DNS entry pinning acm.org to the decommissioned address. No routing changes were needed on my side — the cooperative chain isolated the fault to AS1's local DNS misconfiguration.
